### What's New in Oto v1.2.3:
- **Local Folder-Centric Audio**: Switched from phone-wide auto audio scanning to pure folder selection via system folder picker. Only songs in your chosen music folders are indexed.
- **System Performance Overhaul**: Eliminated background recomposition storms and disk contention, making touch interactions, scrolling, and page transitions noticeably faster and smoother.
- **Instant Cold Start**: Added persistent migration caching to eliminate redundant database checks on startup.
- **SQLite Optimization**: Enforced effective WAL and normal synchronous mode via native SQL execution to prevent UI thread I/O lockups.
