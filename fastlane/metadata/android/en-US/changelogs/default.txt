### What's New in Oto v1.0.8:
- **Local Playback & Offline Mode Fix**: Local audio files (`content://` / `file://`) now read directly from storage without hitting `playerCache` or falling back to online YouTube lookups.
- **No False "Stream failed: no internet connection" on Local Songs**: Local playback errors never lock onto network retry loops when Offline Mode is enabled or WiFi/data is off, and report accurate file/permission errors cleanly.
- **Crossfade & Multi-Track Stability Fix**: Fixed crossfade player swap deadlocks, preserved shuffle order across crossfades, prevented `isCrossfading` / `STATE_IDLE` lockups so playback never pauses permanently after several songs, and restored direct Play/Resume recovery from idle/error states.
