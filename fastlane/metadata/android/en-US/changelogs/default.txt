### What's New in Oto v1.0.10:
- **Resilient Local Audio Resolution (SAF Tree / Non-ASCII / Kanji Paths)**: Fixed the persistent local playback error (`Failed to determine if primary:... is child of primary:...`) caused by Android SAF `ExternalStorageProvider` canonicalization failures on deep folders or non-ASCII characters (e.g. Japanese Kanji). The new `LocalAudioResolver` transparently resolves document URIs directly to accessible filesystem paths or canonical `MediaStore` URIs.
- **Universal Local Playback Direct Path**: Ensured all local playback triggers (single track, playlist, queue, shuffle, and Hero Carousel gacha roll) resolve local URIs directly through `DefaultMediaSourceFactory`, completely eliminating `DynamicResolvingMediaSource` cancellation races and spurious "stream interrupted" errors.
- **Robust Multi-Strategy Fallback**: Multi-tier resolution (direct File -> MediaStore query by DATA -> MediaStore query by displayName + relativePath/size -> non-tree SAF document URI) ensures local tracks (including Hi-Res FLAC) play immediately without intermittent stalls.
