### What's New in Oto v1.0.11:
- **Android Auto (AA) Support & Voice Commands**: Added native Android Auto interface with browsable media catalogs, driver-friendly player, synced lyrics display on car head units, and Google Assistant voice playback support.
- **Full Song Metadata & Tag Editor**: Integrated comprehensive in-app editor for song title, artist relations, album, release year, custom cover art, and YouTube video link pairing.
- **Crossmix & AudioPass Enhancements**: Refined seamless crossfade transitions and pass blockers handling for pristine gapless listening.
- **Device Interconnectivity & Remote Control**: Seamless pairing and synchronized control between Oto on phone, car, and TV with secure SecretVault credentials.
- **App Behaviour Controls**: Added Pause on Mute toggle and Resume on Bluetooth Connect toggle in App Behaviour settings.
