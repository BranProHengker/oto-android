### What's New in Oto v1.0.7:
- **Android TV Mode & Phone Link**: Added dedicated TV interface + phone-to-TV pairing, two-way library sync, local download streaming, and remote queue controls straight from the player menu.
- **Blend Skips & Crossfade**: Preloads adjacent tracks (configurable ahead count, Data Saver aware) so skips/backs crossfade seamlessly.
- **Per-Song Speed & Pitch**: Option to save playback speed and pitch exclusively for the current song.
- **Storage Flexibility**: Choose any custom folder on internal storage or SD card for downloads and cache.
- **Playlist, Search & Player Upgrades**: Auto-download new playlist songs, in-playlist & in-album search, Recent Searches, Studio Version playback for music videos, Keep Shuffle On, Poster chevron queue access, Discord custom artwork, and preserved Local .lrc File Picker + Smart 3-State Shuffle.
- **Pure Oto Experience**: All upstream Mochy mascot assets, donate prompts, and branding stripped and aligned cleanly with Oto.
