### What's New in Oto v1.0.9:
- **Direct Local MediaSource & Cancellation Race Fix**: Local `content://` and `file://` tracks now bypass `DynamicResolvingMediaSource` completely, eliminating `CancellationException` / `IO_UNSPECIFIED` ("stream interrupted") failures during queue changes, Hero Carousel rolls, and crossfades.
- **MediaStore Fallback & Raw Path Normalization**: Added automatic `MediaStore.getMediaUri` fallback if opening a SAF `content://` URI fails, plus raw filesystem path normalization.
- **Hero Carousel Local Playback Fix**: Local tracks rolled or played from the Hero Carousel now queue locally via `ListQueue` instead of invoking online YouTube Radio lookups.
- **Clear Local Error Diagnostics**: Local playback errors surface the exact underlying cause instead of masking `IO_UNSPECIFIED` as "stream interrupted".
