### What's New in Oto v1.2.0:
- **USB DAC & Audio Engine Optimizations**: Fixes & tuning across USB/Exclusive AudioPass (`usb_pass.cpp`, `exclusive_pass.cpp`), `PrecisionSink`, `FfmpegFallbackRenderer`, `PeakLimiter`, and `SpatialAudioProcessor`.
- **Floating Lyrics / Pill Overhaul**: Rebuilt Floating Lyrics & Floating Pill window with new controls, drag/drop targets, and `NavPill`.
- **Spotify Canvas (`sp_dc`)**: Fetch animated Spotify Canvas backgrounds with customizable canvas priority order.
- **Spotify-Style Queue & New Sleep Timer**: FIFO manual queue ahead of Autoplay (with duplicate track support) and revamped dark Sleep Timer sheet with quick presets and live countdown.
- **In-App Module Browser & Settings Refresh**: Built-in Module Browser, dedicated Now Playing & Playback settings screens, and native FLAC export with embedded tags (`Mp4FlacRemux`, `ExportTags`).
- **Source-Module DASH Fix & OriginOS Support**: Direct DASH segment fetching for third-party modules plus Vivo OriginOS Music Card / Atomic Island integration.
- **Oto Signature Features Preserved & Hardened**: Non-blocking local audio auto-healing (`LocalAudioResolver`), Smart Shuffle (`OFF → ON → SMART`) on zero-ANR `DefaultShuffleOrder`, local `.lrc` sidecar auto-detection & picker, structured playback diagnostics copy, and 100% clean Oto branding (no donate prompts or mascots).
