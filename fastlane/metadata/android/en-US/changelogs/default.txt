### What's New in Oto v1.1.0:
- **Local Audio CJK / Japanese Path Fix**: Resolved SAF (Storage Access Framework) failure on non-ASCII/Kanji folders (`Couldn't play local file: Failed to determine if primary:... is child of primary:...`) with direct POSIX & MediaStore fallbacks and automatic runtime permissions.
- **Shuffle Freeze & ANR Deadlock Eradicated**: Eliminated blocking main-thread database queries during local music shuffle with zero-latency in-memory URI resolution.
- **Crossfade Overlap & Duplicate Play Fix**: Fixed crossfade track truncation and audio volume ducking so outgoing tracks fade smoothly without overlapping next track duplication.
- **Android Auto (AA) Support & Voice Commands**: Native Android Auto media browsing and head unit player.
- **Full Song Metadata & Tag Editor**: Integrated comprehensive in-app editor for song title, artist relations, album, release year, custom cover art, and YouTube video link pairing.
- **LrcRed / Apple Music TTML Lyrics Provider**: Word-by-word synchronized lyrics with romaji/furigana transliterations and translations.
- **Crossmix & AudioPass Enhancements**: Refined seamless crossfade transitions and pass blockers handling for pristine gapless listening.
- **Device Interconnectivity & Remote Control**: Clean pairing and synchronized control between Oto on phone, car, and TV.
- **App Behaviour Controls**: Added Pause on Mute toggle and Resume on Bluetooth Connect toggle in App Behaviour settings.
