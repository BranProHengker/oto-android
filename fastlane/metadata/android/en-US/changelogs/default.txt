### What's New in Oto:
- **Zero Ads & Zero Nagging**: Completely purged all upstream Mochy donation popups, nag dialogs, support sheets, and donation buttons.
- **Clean Profile**: Removed developer showcase cards, contributor sections, and third-party promo links.
- **Engine Improvements (Synced Upstream)**:
  - MicroSD Card support for offline downloads and local library indexing.
  - Download Exporter to export downloaded tracks to local storage.
  - Flagship Landscape mode with custom dual-pane tablet/phone layouts.
  - Media3 FFmpeg fallback audio decoder.
  - Skin shaders and enhanced visual customisation.
- **Oto Signature Features Retained**:
  - Embedded & Sidecar LRC lyric support.
  - Smart 3-State Shuffle (Off / Random / Balanced Smart Shuffle).
  - Pure, lightweight ad-free music player experience.
- **Updater & Branding**: In-app updater and version sheets aligned strictly with Oto releases.
